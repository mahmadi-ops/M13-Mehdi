    Section 1.2 Graph, Domain, and Range of \(\cosh x\) and \(\sinh x\)

    Since \(\sinh x\) and \(\cosh x\) are built from the two exponentials \(\tfrac12 e^x\) and \(\tfrac12 e^{-x}\text{,}\) their graphs are easiest to understand by sketching those exponentials first. See Figure 1.2.1 and Figure 1.2.2.
    🔗
    From the graphs we can read off the following facts.
    🔗
    • The domain of \(\cosh x\) and \(\sinh x\) is \(\R\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    • The range of \(\sinh x\) is \(\R\text{,}\) whereas the range of \(\cosh x\) is \([1,\infty)\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    • Notice that \(\sinh x\) is an odd function, i.e. \(\sinh(-x) = -\sinh(x)\text{,}\) whereas \(\cosh x\) is an even function, i.e. \(\cosh(-x) = \cosh(x)\text{.}\)
